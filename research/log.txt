It is clear WOB was in correspondence with B. Russell while beginning to write the Grammar, both before (1917) [047698.txt] and during (1919) [047699.txt] [047700.txt]. The framing that WOB began the Grammar in response to a request to "restate" Vaihinger's `Als Ob`, but resulted in something very different [047699.txt] is interesting historically, though I doubt this was actually the case; although the `Logical Fictions` articles, the most direct predecessors of the Grammar, are published later, I suspect the core line of thought to have been in development for longer.

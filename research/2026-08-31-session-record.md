**Pseudonymous *Grammar* (1924):** Framed as written by a father (Stephen) for his son (Michael). Noted structure includes “Spades: What happens when we think?” and “Hearts: What happens when we say what we think?” Attribution to Brigstocke is supported by contemporary acknowledgement (bookseller citation of Beveridge thanking W. O. Brigstocke for a copy, 7 July 1924) and by this project’s framing. A copy/excerpt appears among Alfred North Whitehead’s papers (Whitehead Research Library, DOC220). One known presentation copy was inscribed to Walter Ripman (father of Hugh Ripman).

This repository treats the *Grammar* as the nucleus of the WOB Papers Repository / Grammar Project (centennial noted for 2024 in `README.md`).

## 2. Archival correspondence dive

**Question:** Which archives hold correspondence to/from William Osborne Brigstocke?

**Result:** No personal WOB fonds found. Surviving letters appear in others’ archives. Two repositories have **confirmed, catalog-named** correspondence; several others are high-value unverified leads.

### Negative findings (not W. O. Brigstocke / no letters found)

- **NLW / Carmarthenshire Brigstocke estate papers** — Welsh landed family (Blaenpant, Llechdwnni, etc.), not this educator
- **Royal Society** — 18th-c. Owen Brigstocke ↔ Hans Sloane (subscription arrears)
- **Armitt Charlotte Mason archive** — published *Parents’ Review* pieces; no Brigstocke letters listed in CM finding aid surveyed
- **ArchiveGrid / British Library / TNA Discovery** — no personal manuscript collection under his name
- **George Allen & Unwin (Reading)** — archive from 1914; his 1906 Dietzel book was **T. Fisher Unwin**, whose author files are not intact as a single archive
- **Walter Ripman / Rippmann** — known inscribed *Grammar* recipient; no centralized personal papers found that would hold a reply file

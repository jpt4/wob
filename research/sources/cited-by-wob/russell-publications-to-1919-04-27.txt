Bertrand Russell: publications through 27 April 1919

Cutoff: the date of W. O. Brigstocke's letter of 27 April 1919 (047699).
A work is included when its printed date is on or before that day.
Issues dated only by month are included when the month is April 1919 or earlier.

Source: the Bertrand Russell Society's concise bibliography
(https://users.drew.edu/~jlenz/br-bib-books.html,
 https://users.drew.edu/~jlenz/br-bib-articles.html,
 https://users.drew.edu/~jlenz/br-bib-letters.html),
which lists printed books, pamphlets, articles, reviews, and letters
to the editor. It is drawn from Kenneth Blackwell, Harry Ruja, and
Sheila Turcon, A Bibliography of Bertrand Russell (Routledge, 1994),
Nicholas Griffin's bibliography, and The Collected Papers of Bertrand Russell.
Unpublished lectures, private letters, and interviews are not listed.
Reprint notes (PE, ML, LK, and so on) are kept from that bibliography.

047704
W. O. Brigstocke to Bertrand Russell
29 Shakespeare Road, Bedford, 20 October 1919
Autograph letter, one page. Bertrand Russell Archives, RA1 710, box 5.05.

29 Shakespeare Road
Bedford 20. x. 19.

Dear Sir.

Since I saw you I have paid two visits to Germany & Vaihinger came to Leipzig to see me. His teaching has had a very great effect in Germany & has been taken up by workers in various fields such as Law, Religion, Medicine, Physics, Mathematics, Ethics and Education. In fact, he is hailed as the leader of German thought!

I have taken your advice & started to write: the Open Court Co. has accepted a series of articles for the Monist; Mr Jourdain accepted them just before his death. He showed an article about you which I believe has just appeared from which I gathered that he had some measure of odium philosophicum!

Vaihinger has written to me (the letter was 14 days on the way) asking whether you would accept a copy of the Philosophie des Als Ob. He is very old & nearly blind. It would give him great pleasure to send it to you. May I say (I am off again soon) that you will accept it?

Yours sincerely
W. O. Brigstocke.

W. O. Brigstocke to Bertrand Russell
Royal Societies' Club, St James's Street, 5 October 1920
Autograph letter, one page. Bertrand Russell Archives, RA1 710, box 5.05.

Royal Societies' Club
St James's Street S.W.1
5. x. 20.

Dear Mr Russell

Thank you for your letter & your testimonial. It has just come in time. It was very kind of you to bother under the circumstances.

The Oxford Congress was a success, though dull. Many of the speakers were very bad & one had far too much Bergson. The weather was perfect — Oxford on a fine September day or moonlit night is something worth going to even with a congress added.

If the Chinese want a philosopher whose only qualifications are a strong desire to get out of England and to be somewhere else, will you please tell them to send for me? I find it difficult to imagine myself ever making a decent living here without being a humbug: it takes a big man to defy public opinion: but I think there may be places in the world where my unorthodoxy might be an asset.

Thank you again for your kindness.

Yours sincerely
W. O. Brigstocke
